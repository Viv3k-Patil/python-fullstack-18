Mr.IPL-Suresh Raina profile

Suresh Raina represented the new-age Indian cricketer: An attacking left-hander who went for the big shots with impunity and cleared the field with a flourish when at the top of his game, he was also an electric fielder in the circle.
All of this, though, was tempered with an iffy technique against the short ball, which was exploited mercilessly in Test cricket and meant Raina's Test career never reached the heights his limited-overs career did, despite starting promisingly.

Personal Information

  • FullName:Suresh Kumar Raina
  • Birth Date:November 27, 1986
  • Birth Place:: Muradnagar, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, India
  • Batting Style:Left-Handed
  • Bowling Style: Right-arm off-break

Career Highlights

  • Debut: Made his ODI debut in 2005 against Sri Lanka.
  • Test Century:Scored a century on his Test debut against Sri Lanka in 2010.
  • Historic Feat:First Indian batsman to hit a century in all three formats of international cricket (Test, ODI, and T20I).
  • World Cup Champion: Integral part of the victorious Indian squad at the 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup.
  • IPL Legend:Second-highest run-getter for the Chennai Super Kings and a key figure in multiple IPL title wins.
