MS Dhoni

MS DHONI

Mahendra Singh Dhoni is a former Indian cricketer and captain of the Indian national team.

Personal Information

  • Born: 7 July 1981, Ranchi, India
  • Full name: Mahendra Singh Dhoni
  • Nickname: Mahi, Thala
  • Role: Wicket-keeper batsman
  • International debut: 23 December 2004 (ODI), 2 January 2005 (Test)
  • Retirement: 15 August 2020 (International cricket)
